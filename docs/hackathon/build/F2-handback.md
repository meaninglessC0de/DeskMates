Also `mimeapps.list` and xfce `WebBrowser=firefox`, `update-alternatives` for `x-www-browser` and `gnome-www-browser` (already Firefox on this image), and a managed Chromium policy that stops its "Make Chromium faster" popup (and the bookmark bar). Chromium stays installed. Verified by looking at frames from a hired desktop: one Firefox window, maximised (`_NET_WM_STATE_MAXIMIZED_*`, 1280 by 749), no banner, no first-run page; a Chromium opened by hand showed no popup. Chromium itself still opens small and left (the prompt tells employees Firefox only).
