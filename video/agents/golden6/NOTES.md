# Golden 6: re-recorded takes, full answers, varied swooshes (15 Nov)

User asked (after golden): more variety of swooshes; agents glitched between what they said and had no time to say anything useful; re-record the meeting; make every crossfade and motion decision make sense; rebuild from scratch if needed.

## What was done
- **All takes re-recorded** with Recordly on a freshly recreated demo team (same story; the old desktops were gone, so desks, call, meeting, approval and Team view were all re-shot so the whole video matches): `video/public/media/raw6/`.
- **Full answers.** The build session fixed the call mind (token cap 400 -> 1200, "two or three complete sentences", no app names). Meeting questions were rewritten to ask for something useful ("Maya, how's the checkout fix?", "Jonas, what did you find on pricing?", "Ines, which onboarding should we ship?"). Each question was sent only after the previous answer had finished (the app's own "Speaking" indicator), so nobody is cut off.
- **No glitches.** The meeting is one continuous take. The only jumps skip the app's empty switch frames (hidden under the share-switch animations) and two sentences we don't use (Jonas's unfinished third sentence, Ines's second), dissolving between matching frames. No frozen faces except one 1-second hold on Jonas's strip tile while his skipped sentence would show. The app's meeting clock is covered because it would visibly jump at those two joins.
- **Maya's call** ends on her last word and the face is a still as it flies back, so she never mouths unheard words.
- **Truthful approval.** Approving Maya's card really pushed her branch; her desk's status then flips from "Needs you · Push fix/checkout-discount" to "Done" (a second Desks recording made right after the approval).
- **Swooshes.** 12 Mixkit swooshes (licence approved by the user), one kind per motion: long sweep for the windows flying onto the desks, impact for the drop, quick air sweeps for desk-to-desk moves, windy transitions for big camera pull-backs, air woosh into the call, cinematic swoosh as faces fly into the meeting, small sweeps on share switches, wind as faces fly home, short wind on the message bubbles, tech slide on the approval card, quick accent on Priya's notification, futuristic pass as the desks become the logo. Each is aligned by its measured peak.
- Length: 2:15 (the meeting needs the time for complete answers).
