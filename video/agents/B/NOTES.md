# Edit B: Deskmates demo, 2:00, 1920x1080, 30 fps

Files: `out/deskmates-B.mp4` (final, -16 LUFS), `out/deskmates-B-preview.mp4`. Source: `index.ts`, `Root.tsx`, `Main.tsx` (the edit), `parts.tsx` (components), `geo.ts` (measured geometry, timeline, spoken lines), `tools/render.mjs`, `tools/audio.py`, `tools/lines.json`. Built from the raw recordings only; no earlier composition, clip list or derived file was opened.

## How it is built
One "world" of 1920x1080 under a single camera (scale and pan only). The world is Sam's Mac (wallpaper, menu bar) with the Deskmates window on it (1536x960, the 2560x1600 recordings at 0.6). Everything that changes focus is a camera move or an object moving between places.
- **0:00 to 0:24, the pile.** The Mac is drawn (motion graphics). Four of the seven windows are the agents' real windows cropped from the Desks recording (Maya's terminal, Priya's launch post, Jonas's pricing chart, Ines's onboarding designs); the code editor, "zsh (4)", "agent chat" and "agents.log" are drawn (the log uses the run's real activity lines; the chat shows real activity lines too). Windows shake from 14.3 s (about half the amplitude of the earlier cut, ramped in over 1 s), only once agents.log appears. The cursor glides to the Deskmates mark in the menu bar and clicks; the menu is drawn (only "Show desks" is a real item; "Open Deskmates" and "Quit" are placeholders).
- **0:24 drop.** The four crops fly onto the same windows on their live desks in one eased move each (ease in-out cubic, 2.1 s, slight arc, tilt to zero, staggered Maya, Priya, Jonas, Ines) while the camera pulls back with the same easing and the drawn windows shrink away. The recording underneath is pixel-identical at landing, so the crop is simply removed.
- **Tour.** The camera flies to each desk on the word that names it (timed from silence detection on the narration), with a small pull-back arc between desks, then back to the grid.
- **Faces.** The app's own face pop (take 24.0 s) is left as recorded, on "And every agent gets a face".
- **Call (match move).** Maya's desk tile grows into the call's desktop panel and her face card grows into the call's face tile (the card still blends into the live call face in the middle 20% of the move); the other desks fade into the window's dark ground, the call UI fades in once the two objects are in place. The move reverses on the way out. No dissolve between layouts.
- **Meeting.** The four face cards fly (staggered, small arc) to the four meeting tiles, seated as the logo; at the end the faces fly from the spotlight strip back to their cards while Ines's spotlight desktop shrinks into her desk tile. Each app spotlight swap (Maya 31.6 s, Jonas 43.1 s, Ines 54.7 s in the take) is played from the recording at 0.4x. Between swaps the static spotlight is joined with 0.35 s dissolves between nearly identical frames (to skip Maya's second answer, Jonas's "open in Firefox" and the waiting). The app's own caption row is covered with a window-coloured bar; my subtitles sit below the window.
- **Then.** Priya's and Jonas's real messages fly desk to desk as bubbles. Maya's approval notification drops in softly, grows into the real thread with the real card and the real Approve click (take 3.3 s lands on Sam's "Approve."), then shrinks back to her desk. The Team view (real numbers, real question and answer) slides up and down; the wait for the answer is a dissolve between nearly identical spinner frames. Priya's notification slides in softly with her real line.
- **End card.** The grid dips to dark, the four tiles of the mark build in (from the real SVG), the wordmark, the tagline (on screen as the burned-in caption of the last narration line, so the sentence is not shown twice), the four faces and the "Built with ..." line, fade to black.

## Real footage versus graphics
Real: every desktop, face, call, meeting, approval, Team view, the audio of Maya, Jonas and Ines (call 10.14 to 15.92 s; meeting 24.20 to 31.68, 48.32 to 52.32, 73.40 to 76.74), messages quoted. Motion graphics: the Mac (wallpaper, menu bar, menu, cursor), drawn pile windows, flying objects (they are crops/stills of the recordings), notification cards, message bubbles, end card, subtitles.

## Audio
Narrator and Sam: the existing `n*.wav` and `s*.wav`. Music: Mixkit "Motivating Mornings" re-cut on its beat grid (123 BPM): a softened quiet part for 0 to 13 s, its own breakdown (48 to 63.76 s) as the tension from 13 to 24 s, the big section from 24.0 s (its 63.76 s hit lands on the click) looped on bar boundaries, its outro ending at 2:00. The riser is synthesized (filtered noise and a rising tone), fades in with a cubic curve from 16.2 s to 24.0 s, joined by Mixkit "whoosh impact" whose peak sits on the drop, plus a synthesized sub boom and two soft clicks. Mixkit "air woosh" (lowpassed) on big moves only; soft chime (lowpassed, about -17 dB) for the approval card and a softer one for Priya's; tiny pops for the face pop and the two bubbles. Music ducked 10 dB under every line (12 ms-style smoothing: 0.12 s attack, 0.45 s release), loudnorm I=-16, TP=-1.5, fade over the last 2 s.

## Rules bent or not met (plain)
- The Mac is drawn, not a real recording of Sam's Mac; the brief has no such recording.
- Script timings moved: the meeting ended earlier than the script (real take lengths) so the end card comes at about 1:52; the call line is placed after the face pop; "Maya, show me the fix" rings Maya's tile a little before she answers (the app's own "Speaking" label).
- After the meeting the desks show the earlier recorded state (Ines's "Chosen: Version B" badge is visible in the spotlight but not on her desk tile, which is from the Desks take). The desk recording ends at 42.6 s; later desk views loop its last 7 s with 0.35 s near-identical dissolves.
- The spotlight strip faces fly with a still of the face (not live), a blink-sized pop at the swap.
- During the silent part of the spotlight takes, an agent's lips may move in the strip (speech we do not use).
- Overlays use the app's look (neutral palette, the four desk colours, Figtree if installed, else system font) but are hand-built, not Astryx components, to keep the render deterministic. No other UI library is used. `astryx doctor` was not run (nothing is an app front end).
- Firefox is never mentioned in audio, subtitles or overlays (it appears only as the real window title bars inside the recordings, which cannot be changed).
- The approval card is "Push fix/checkout-discount" (the real first card), not "Publish with git push".
